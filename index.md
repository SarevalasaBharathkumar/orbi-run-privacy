# Orbi Run - Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 19 November 2026
**App:** Orbi Run (Android package `com.orbiaskswhy.orbirun`)
**Developer:** Orbi Asks Why
**Contact:** sarevalasab@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the Orbi Run game ("the app") uses, why, and what choices you have.
The short version: Orbi Run has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases, and it does not collect your name,
email address, location, contacts, photos or microphone. It sends only anonymous crash and performance reports
so that we can fix bugs and make the game run smoothly on more phones.

## 1. Information that stays on your device

The app saves your game progress on your phone only: your best distance, coins, keys, chosen outfit,
sound settings and the picture-quality level the app picked for your phone. This information is stored in the app's
private storage and is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## 2. Crash and performance reports (sent over the internet)

The app uses an error-reporting service, **Sentry** (Functional Software, Inc.), to receive anonymous technical reports.
A report is sent when the app crashes or hits an error, and also as a short summary after about a minute of play.
A report can contain:

- the app version and the game world being played;
- your phone's make and model, Android version, processor and graphics chip, amount of memory and screen size;
- the language and time zone setting of the phone;
- technical details of a crash (which part of the program failed) and a short excerpt of the game's technical log;
- frame-rate figures (how smoothly the game ran, and how many slow frames there were);
- a random installation number that the reporting tool creates on your phone. It is not your Android advertising ID, not
  your phone's serial number or IMEI, and it is not linked to your name or any account.

Like any internet connection, the reporting service receives the IP address of your connection when a report arrives.
We do not use it to identify you.

**Why we use this information:** to find and fix crashes, and to improve performance on slower phones.
We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not combine it with any other data about you.

**Where it goes:** reports are processed by Sentry on our behalf and stored on servers in the European Union (Germany).
Sentry acts as our service provider. Its own privacy policy is at <https://sentry.io/privacy/>.

**How long it is kept:** reports are kept for a limited period (set by the reporting service's plan, typically 30 to 90 days)
and then deleted automatically.

**Security:** reports are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

## 3. What the app does not do

- It has no sign-in and does not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
- It shows no advertising and uses no advertising ID.
- It has no in-app purchases; everything in the game is earned by playing.
- It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
- It does not track you across other apps or websites.
- The only Android permission it asks for is **Internet access**, which is used only to send the reports described above.

## 4. Children

Orbi Run is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, please contact us and we will delete any related reports.

## 5. Your choices and rights

Because the reports are anonymous, we usually cannot tell which reports came from you. Depending on where you live
(for example under the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to ask what data we hold, to have it deleted, or to object to its use.
To ask, email us at sarevalasab@gmail.com with your phone model and the approximate date and time you played, and we will look for
matching reports and delete them. Uninstalling the app also stops all reporting from that phone, and reinstalling creates a new random number.

## 6. Third-party material

The game uses pictures and 3D models from public sources (NASA, ESA, Solar System Scope under CC BY 4.0, and Poly Haven under CC0).
The full credits are shown in the app under Settings.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above. The latest version is always at this address.
